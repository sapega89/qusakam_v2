# Kusakam: the game's pillars

Sapega confirmed these on 2026-10-23 (D585). Read this before any game-design call: a feature that serves none of the
pillars is cut, and a conflict between two features is settled in pillar order.

## The player fantasy (D579)

A **cowardly but brave little lizard villager** from the edge of the worlds. He was raised by his shaman grandfather
and finds a **paladin's** powers in himself, after the sleeping barrier lets the bandits in. Full quote: [[lore]].

## The four pillars

1. **Hard, readable combat** (D580, D581, D584).
   - The core loop is strike, dodge and parry, and it comes first.
   - Every blow is telegraphed: white means block, yellow means parry, red means roll (D573).
   - Each blow has weight: hitstop, shake, sound.
   - A death reads as "I saw it and didn't roll".
   - Growth eases the game but never makes it easy.
2. **Locations with secrets** (D458, D562, D565, D583).
   - Three demo locations, each with a main path and branches: a little cave, an attic, a cellar.
   - Every branch ends in a chest, a piece of story or a shortcut.
   - Nothing lies free on the road. Paths open with keys and NPC talks.
3. **A build through profession and skills** (D535, D570, D530).
   - A permanent oath.
   - 19 skills a tab, with caps so that not everything fits.
   - 8 hotbar slots, attributes, weapons.
   - Choices cost something.
4. **Companions** (D515, D523–D525).
   - One assist on the field at a time, long and beautiful, with a shared cooldown.
   - Each companion has its own role.

## Rules that follow

- **Pacing** (D582): new enemies come alone first, then in pairs. Crowds are rare and hard. The rhythm is fight → quiet → secret → boss.
- **Genre** (D563): a 2D side-scrolling action RPG with souls-like combat, like Tails of Iron. It is not an
  ability-gated metroidvania.
- **Proof:** a combat change is judged in a real fight capture, not only by a green test (D580).
