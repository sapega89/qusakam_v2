# Skill icons: what each one does and how to draw it

Sapega asked for this on 2026-10-23: «зроби для кожної іконки опис, що вона робить… залежить від того, що воно має робити в описі скіла D&D або Pathfinder, і тоді буде ясно, як малювати».

The skill shapes come from **D&D 5e SRD 5.1** (CC BY 4.0) and **Pathfinder 2e** (ORC) (D285). This note borrows only the image, not the text.

## Drawing rules

The rules come from D550, D599, D601 and D602:
- Pixel art like the hero's, 48×48, transparent background, no frame.
- The element that carries the meaning sits in the centre.
- At most three colours. Active: ivory with gold. Passive: ivory with steel-blue. Ultimate: gold with crimson. All with a dark outline.
- An icon is a **base** plus an **effect**, built from the kit. Hands are always Kusakam's three-fingered hand in a priest's glove. A figure is a small faceless figure.
- Never Kusakam himself. Sapega has to recognise the skill without a caption.

## Kit

| Bases | Effects |
|---|---|
| Sword, dagger, mace/hammer, halberd, axe, shield, bow, staff | Fire, dark fire, lightning, holy light (rays), sunburst |
| Gloved hand: open palm, fist, two praying hands, a grasping hand, a hand raised in an oath | Rune, blood (drops), whirl (sand, wind), impact (flash and stars), sparks |
| Figure: strike, dash, leap with a knee, spin, kneeling, shout, standing guard | Aura ring, dome or barrier, sigil on the ground, chain, crack, ripple |
| Heart, chalice, scales, plate of armour | Motion lines, rising motes |

### Order

1. Each asset gets 3 candidates in the approved pixel style. Each is drawn **once**, in ivory with gold, on transparent, 48×48 (`comfy_icons.py --assets`).
2. Sapega picks one per asset. The pick is copied to `SampleProject/Assets/UI/Icons/kit/<asset>.png`, and from then on it is an asset.
3. Colours are swapped in code, not redrawn. The gold in the three-colour palette maps to steel-blue for passives and to crimson for ultimates (D602).
4. Each skill has a recipe in `Resources/Data/skill_icons.json`: the base, the effect, their offsets and scales. A composer builds every icon and colour variant from the kit, so the same shield is the same everywhere.
