## B205: the MetSys sample's debug UI on the M map

`SampleProject/Game.tscn` `UI/MapWindow` was the MetSys sample's window. Three sample-only parts sat on it:
the `Label` «Press D to toggle delta vector», the `Percent` label (`"%03d%%"` of `MetSys.get_explored_ratio()`, which
showed «0%» before the first update and «001%» after) and `TopDraw`, which drew the "delta vector" arrow from the last
save point. D toggled it, and D is also `move_right` in the game.

Removed (the real map stays: M opens and closes it, arrows pan it, the hero is paused while it is open):
- `Game.tscn`: the `Percent`, `Label` and `TopDraw` nodes, the `PercentLabel` script, the label's `StyleBoxFlat`,
  the `TopDraw.draw` connection, and from the `MapWindow` script the D branch, `_on_top_draw()`,
  `reset_starting_coords()`, `show_delta` and `starting_coords` (`offset` is now local to `update_offset()`).
- Its callers: `Game.reset_map_starting_coords()` and its call in `Game._ready()` (`Game.gd`), `Portal.gd`, `SavePoint.gd`.
- The dead msgid «Press D to toggle delta vector» from `en.po` and `uk.po`.

The World Map tab (D458) never used any of it. `verify_world_map` and `verify_game_menu` pass.

Test: `verify_world_map.gd` [19]. The map window has no `Label`, no `TopDraw` / `Percent`, no `show_delta` and no
`reset_starting_coords`. M opens it with its MapView, D changes nothing, and M closes it and frees the hero.

## B206: «Hey, wind…» stayed on after a room change

The layer is the **monologue speech bubble** (D178 / D737), not the D110 dialogue window. `Canyon_Intro.dqd` has
only KUSAKAM lines, so `DialogueManager.start_dialogue()` plays it with `_play_bubbles()`, which adds the bubble as a
**child of the Player**. The Player lives in `Game.tscn` and outlives the room, so `Game.load_room()` swapped the
Canyon for DesertRoad with the bubble still on the hero. Its line timer is not `process_always`, so it also waited out
the black screen. The capture script changes rooms in the middle of the intro, which is how the line got into `saving.png`.

Fix (`SampleProject/Scripts/Systems/DialogueManager.gd`):
- `_play_bubbles()` connects `Game.room_swapping` to `bubble.queue_free` (one-shot). The room that started the
  monologue is going, so its bubble goes with it. A freed bubble drops this connection on its own, so a monologue that
  ended normally does not touch the next room change.
- `_on_bubble_gone()` was already the reset for "the scene went in the middle of a monologue". It clears
  `_bubble_playing`, the cloud-talk flag, the queue and the pending triggers. It now also frees the hero
  (`_unpause_player_after_dialogue()`): after a room change he is still in the game, and the D737 hold has to end.

The cloud talks the cutscenes play (`Canyon_AbductionCutscene`, `in_cloud`) are awaited before their teleports, so
the swap cannot cut them off. NPC barks (`say_in_passing`) hang on NPCs, so they leave with the room already.

Test: `verify_dialogue_triggers.gd` [5]. The Canyon intro plays in a bubble, then `game.load_room(DesertRoad)`.
After the change there is no bubble on the hero, the dialogue manager is free, and the hero is free. The next monologue
plays and ends normally. Without the fix: `RESULT:4 FAIL` (no bubble / monologue over / hero free / ends normally).
