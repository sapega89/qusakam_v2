# Cloud report: combat-enemies (D504 enemy side, D494)

**Summary**
- **Done:** colour tells 0.45 s ahead, each with its own sound (white = block, yellow = parry, red = dodge), on every enemy and boss attack. A parry works only on yellow melee strikes and yellow arrows. Boss hazards (wave, fireball, pillar) are their own damage source, so they can be neither blocked nor parried (B133). A parried melee strike opens a 1.0 s riposte: the next attack is an instant heavy hit, ×3 damage, that breaks any poise. Once a tell shows, the swing finishes unless poise breaks; bosses ignore knockback (B134). Every swing ends in a recovery pose. At most two enemies wind up at once. Melee enemies keep their distance, step in to strike and back off. Bosses pick moves from a pattern (distance, last move, phase 2 pattern) and walk to a preferred range between moves. Boss timers stop on pause (B135). Enemies notice the hero only on their own floor or in line of sight, and walkers stop at ledges and walls (B139). Dead code is removed (B140). D494 numbers are below.
- **Not done:** the jackal's leap over an ally, flyers, the shield-bearer, and the room-content group rules (critique §B rows not in this prompt). Regular enemies keep D265's random combo length: only the boss combos are fixed. Healing as a punish trigger waits for the potion work (cloud/combat-player).
- **D-IDs for the register:** D573–D578 (below).
- **B rows fixed:** B133, B134, B135, B139, B140.
- **Full suite:** one run of 99 tests: 97 PASS, 2 FAIL. `verify_combat_flow` [2] was flaky: the jackal's lunge hit a parked enemy's body. `verify_road_sections` checked `is_on_floor` on enemies that were already fighting. Both tests are fixed: [2] parks the other enemies, and the floor check allows 20 frames. Re-runs: combat_flow ALL PASS ×4, road_sections ALL PASS.

## D-ID text (for `figma_decision_register.md`)

| D573 | ROUTINE CALL | **What each tell colour means, as built** (D504; `cloud/combat-enemies`). `EnemyLogic.TELL_LEAD = 0.45` s before the hit, the weapon glints and `EventBus.enemy_tell_shown` plays a sound (`tell_block`: high metal tick; `tell_parry`: high bell; `tell_dodge`: low plate thud; `sound_events.json`). The parry window is still the last `PARRY_WINDOW` (0.25 s) before the hit, so the glint warns and is no longer the deadline. **White (block):** a block holds it; a block in the parry window is still only a block. **Yellow (parry):** a block in the window parries it; a later block holds it, unless the hit would guard-break (stagger ≥ 0.6 s, as before), so a heavy yellow hit is parried or eaten. **Red (dodge):** neither block nor parry holds it; it hits in full and staggers. The colour is decided in `PlayerCombat.guard_against` (`DefaultEnemy.incoming_tell()`, `Arrow.tell`). The direction locks at the tell (it used to lock at 0.25 s). This amends D200 ("no parry colours") and D264. |
| D574 | ROUTINE CALL | **Tell per enemy and per move** (D504; `cloud/combat-enemies`). `EnemyStats.tell` / `finisher_tell`: bandit yellow, then a red finisher; jackal white, then a yellow finisher; scorpion red; archer yellow (arrows can be parried, D384). Bosses: each move has `tell` (charge, slam, fireball, pillar: red) and the combo has `tells` per swing. Brass Collector: yellow, red (phase 2: yellow, yellow, red). | D575 | ROUTINE CALL | **Parry limits and the riposte** (D504, B133; `cloud/combat-enemies`). Boss hazards pass themselves as the damage source (`BossFireball`, `BossPillar`, and the slam's shockwave node), so `guard_against` returns nothing and the boss is never staggered by a parry from afar. Hazards no longer stagger the hero either: `Player.take_damage` staggers only from enemies and arrows, and `Player.gd` was not touched. Riposte: parrying a `DefaultEnemy` (not an arrow, and not with a bow) sets `PlayerCombat.riposte_left = RIPOSTE_WINDOW` (1.0 s). The next attack or heavy press from READY is `_riposte`: the HeavyStrike frames at once with no wind-up, `RIPOSTE_DAMAGE` ×3, `RIPOSTE_POISE` 999 ("ignores poise": it breaks any poise, the boss's included), heavy knockback, then `heavy_recovery`. A stagger, a cancel or leaving the tree closes the window. |
| D576 | ROUTINE CALL | **Committed swings, recovery, token, spacing** (D504, B134, critique §B; `cloud/combat-enemies`). After the tell, `EnemyLogic.committed` is true: knockback and stuns no longer break the swing; only `stagger` (poise break, parry) does. Before the tell, knockback still breaks it (D208). Bosses have `knockback_immune`: no push from hits, the poise-break stagger included. After every series the enemy holds its strike frame for `EnemyStats.recovery` s (`EnemyView.hold_recovery`), then backs off `back_off` px, facing the hero (bandit 80, jackal 120, scorpion 0). The back-off times out after `back_off / speed + 0.4` s when an ally blocks the way. Attack token: `MAX_ATTACKERS = 2` enemies hold it from the first wind-up until the recovery starts; bosses neither take nor count it. Between attacks a melee enemy holds `attack_range + WAIT_GAP` (40 px), shuffling ±12 px at half speed. It steps in only when it is ready and a token is free, or at once when the hero starts a heavy or a cast within 1.5× reach. The archer engages from its own `attack_range`. |
| D577 | ROUTINE CALL | **Boss move patterns** (D504; `cloud/combat-enemies`). `BossEnemy.pattern` / `pattern_phase2` are queues of move kinds. `BossLogic.pick_move` takes the next kind in the queue that fits the distance and phase and is not the last move. A repeat is allowed only when nothing else fits; the queue restarts on a phase change. When nothing fits, the boss walks in. Between moves it walks to `preferred_range` (±30 px) at 0.6× speed. Brass: `combo, slam, combo, charge`; phase 2: `combo, charge, slam, combo, slam`; preferred 60 px. Djinn: `fireball, pillar, combo, fireball, pillar`; phase 2: `pillar, fireball, combo, pillar, fireball`; preferred 300 px. `weight` is gone. Combo length is fixed by `tells`. Phase 2 adds a swing to each combo and a second fireball and side pillars, as before. Provisional, for Sapega's play. |
| D578 | ROUTINE CALL | **Noticing and ledges** (B139; `cloud/combat-enemies`). `EnemyLogic.can_notice`: feet within ±64 px (`NOTICE_DY`), or a clear ray from enemy to hero (enemies and hero excluded). It gates the detection circle, the idle check and the chase. An unseen hero during a chase counts as lost (3 s, then home), and the enemy stands still meanwhile. `can_step(dir)`: no wall within 8 px of the body's edge, and floor no deeper than 16 px under the feet. It is used by every walk (chase, step in, spacing, back-off, home) and replaces the archer's own check. An enemy that cannot get home (knocked over a ledge) makes its current spot its new home. |
