## D766 (ROUTINE CALL): the World Map is a canvas (`cloud/map-pan`)
«The World Map is a canvas (`cloud/map-pan`). Sapega 2026-10-27: «А ми зможемо гуляти мишкою по карті світу? Якось переміщати її, як в Octopath Traveler» → yes, on both levels. **Camera:** screen = offset + point × zoom (`map_camera.gd`). Level 1 moves the menu's own illustrated background (`base_menu.tscn → MapBackground`, group `menu_map_background`) and the pins inside the content area x 320–1920, y 0–1016. The pins ride with the map but keep their size, and they are clipped at the sidebar. Level 2 moves `Grid` inside the existing `Rooms` frame (340–1480 × 160–990). Cells, names and icons scale with the map. The title, the location card, the legend and the new room card stay fixed. **Zoom:** the minimum is the fit. On level 1 that is the smallest scale at which the parchment still covers the content area (0.94). On level 2 it is the whole location in the frame (≤ 1, as D715). The maximum is `MAP_ZOOM_MAX` 2.5, where 1 = the Figma frame / the 150×84 cell. One wheel notch is ×1.15 and zooms around the cursor. The triggers and Q / E zoom at ×2 per second around the centre of the view. **Pan:** a left-button drag follows the cursor 1:1. On release the map glides at the mouse's speed (at most 3000 px/s), slowing as e^(−6t), so it travels about v / 6. There is no glide if the mouse rested for more than 0.1 s before release. WASD and the right stick (dead zone 0.2) pan at 900 px/s. On level 2 the arrows, D-pad and left stick pan too. On level 1 they keep choosing pins (D715), and the view slides to a chosen pin that is within 120 px of the edge. **Edges:** content larger than the view is clamped to its edges; smaller content is centred. A glide that reaches an edge stops there, with no bounce. **Opening:** at the approved frames' scale (1), centred on the hero as far as the edges allow. On level 1 the Canyon pin is near the right edge, so the opening view equals the approved frame. Level 2 opens at the natural cell size, centred on the hero icon. **Hover / click:** a pointer over a pin selects it (gold border and card, as with left / right). A press-and-release that travels ≤ `MAP_DRAG_SLOP` 6 px is a click and opens the pin (a locked pin stays shut, as before). Anything longer is a drag. On level 2 the room under the pointer gets a 4 px `MAP_GOLD` border and a room card at (1500, 160) above the legend. The card shows the room title and «Скрині a/b», or «?» and «бачив вхід, ще не заходив» for a seen room. With keys or a pad, the room under the centre of the view is highlighted. On opening, that is the hero's room. **Keys kept:** Q / E (menu_prev_tab / menu_next_tab) were unused on this tab, so they zoom. LB / RB, which are bound to the same actions, zoom as well. The bottom hint adds «Тягни або стік — рухати карту. Коліщатко або Q / E — масштаб.» The background goes back to the frame when the tab is left (Inventory and the other tabs share it).»

## What was built
- `SampleProject/Scripts/Menus/Game/map_camera.gd` (new, preloaded): zoom, offset, clamp/centre, `zoom_at`, `pan`, glide `step`, `reveal` for key-chosen pins.
- `SampleProject/Scripts/Menus/Game/metsys_map_component.gd`: `world_camera` / `room_camera`; `_mouse_input` (drag, click vs drag by travel, wheel, hover); `_process` (stick / WASD / arrows / triggers / Q-E, glide); `pin_center`, `pin_at`, `room_at`, `_set_hovered_room` (room highlight in `_draw_cells` and the room card); the background is reset in `set_map_active(false)`.
- `metsys_map_component.tscn`: `Pins` became a clipped 320–1920 × 0–1016 area (pins no longer draw over the sidebar once panned). New `Level2/RoomCard` (`MapCard` / `MapCardTitle` / `MapCardText`).
- `base_menu.tscn`: `MapBackground` is in group `menu_map_background`. The map takes the one whose parent is its own ancestor, so the shop and crafting base menus are not touched.
- `UITokens.gd`: `MAP_ZOOM_MAX`, `MAP_ZOOM_STEP`, `MAP_ZOOM_RATE`, `MAP_PAN_SPEED`, `MAP_STICK_DEADZONE`, `MAP_DRAG_SLOP`, `MAP_GLIDE_*`, `MAP_FOLLOW_RATE`, `MAP_REVEAL_MARGIN`, `MAP_ROOM_HOVER_BORDER`. None of them are theme values, so the `.tres` is unchanged.
- `en.po` / `uk.po`: the pan hint, «Chests %d/%d» / «Скрині %d/%d».
