# Cloud report: progression (D447, D488, D492)

**Summary**
- **D488 done:** `XPManager.MAX_LEVEL` is now 20. XP past the cap is not gained, and the HUD XP row, the XP bar and the Status "next level" all show **MAX**. The Mayor Hall door (`City/MayorHallGate`) stays shut until `player_state.oath` is set. Two gate-guard NPCs stand at the door; the right one says a [ЧЕРНЕТКА] line in a bubble (en + uk), and the objective points to the oath keepers. **Not done:** the porch art (later, per the task).
- **D447 behaviour done:** using a save point, the hero sits in the `Meditate` pose, and the save screen opens 0.8 s later. Closing the screen stands him up. **Not done:** the meditation-spot art. The blue "S" circle stays until the art arrives.
- **D492 done, except the new spawn groups:** ordinary fights (the 3 Desert Road sections and the 3 Laboratory corridors, `RoomEnemySpawner.respawns`) come back on meditation or when the hero re-enters their region. Story fights and bosses never come back. The XP curve is now a per-level table: straight through the Laboratory ends at **level 16**, and two more rounds of the corridors reach **20**. The "standing group in every room" part of D492 is **not built**, because it was not in this task.
- **D-IDs for the register:** D523–D526 (below). **B rows fixed:** none.
- **Full suite:** _see the last section_.

| D525 | ROUTINE CALL | **How D492's respawn rule is built** (`cloud/progression`; amends D152 and D306 "a won fight never comes back"). The regions are data in `Resources/Data/regions.json` (prologue: Canyon, Village, ElderHouse; desert_road: DesertRoad; city: CityGates, City, MayorHall; laboratory: LabCells, Laboratory, LabBoss, LaboratoryOutside), read by `Scripts/Systems/Regions.gd`. `RoomEnemySpawner.respawns` (default false) marks an ordinary fight. It is true for `Fight_Dunes`, `Fight_Mesa`, `Fight_Caravan`, `Fight_Upper`, `Fight_Middle` and `Fight_Lower`. Story fights (`EnemySpawner` in Village, DesertRoad and CityGates) and bosses (not spawners) are untouched. When a respawning fight starts, it registers in `room_state[room]["ordinary_fights"]` as {key: region}, so the list is saved with the slot. `Game.reset_ordinary_fights(region)` erases those keys: `Game.meditate()` erases all of them, and `Game.load_room` erases the target region's keys when the hero crosses into a different region. A spawner already live in the open room stops recording when its fight is reset, so a fight left mid-way after meditating starts fresh next time. Moving between rooms of the same region keeps the state (D152 within a region). **A won fight stays won for the story:** when the reset erases a fight that was cleared, it leaves `<key>_won` in `room_state`, and `RoomEnemySpawner.fight_won()` is `fight_cleared()` or that mark. `Laboratory.is_fight_cleared` now uses `fight_won()`. Without this, meditating at `SavePoint_PreBoss` brought the upper and middle corridors back and `lab_corridors_complete` could never be reached. **A consequence to know:** `SavePoint_PreBoss` stands west of `Fight_Lower`'s zone, so after a save (and so after every boss retry from Game Over) the hero fights through the lower corridor again before the djinn. That is the Tails of Iron and Hollow Knight pattern D492 asks for. If it is too much before a boss, the save point can move east of that zone (D173 placement call). |
