# dust

Drifting dust motes, 6 frames of 64×64, tileable. Drawn procedurally by `Scripts/Tools/comfy_decor.py` (`dust()`), original work, not AI-generated; colours are the sand / haze tones of the generated backdrops. See `../README.md`.

**A7 redraw** (2026-10-28, D813 «прибрати»): the old sheet was an even scatter of identical 1–2 px dots, each circling on
its own, and read as noise or stars rather than dust in the wind. Now three sizes — 1-px specks, soft round 2×2 motes
with fainter corners, and 3 wisps with a 7-px tail along their own circle — all swirling the same way (a whirl, not a
swarm), each twinkling with its own phase; alpha in four steps. Same 6 frames of 64×64, same colours, still a seamless
loop and tileable. Still drawn by the script, not AI-generated.
