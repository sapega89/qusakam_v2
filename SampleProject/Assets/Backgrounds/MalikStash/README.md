# Malik's Stash planes (M6d-1)

**AI-generated.** Made with `design/shots/candidates/m6d1/m6d1.py` on top of `Scripts/Tools/comfy_backdrop.py`, using
**Qwen-Image** (Alibaba Qwen, Apache 2.0) with the Qwen-Image-Lightning LoRA (lightx2v, Apache 2.0). Brought down ×3 to
the game's pixel and a few colours (octree); the wall and the shaft are darkened toward violet. The floor is
`../Cellar/gen_floor.png`. A Steam release has to disclose AI-generated content. Review:
`design/shots/m6d1_lab_rooms.png` (frames 6–7).

The room (`Maps/MalikStash.tscn`): secret, 2 cells (1152 × 320 art = 1728 × 480 world) at [12,4] on layer 2, right
under the cells; floor y 384 world (art row 256), camera x 0…576 art, y fixed. «There's something under the loose slab
in my cell» (Malik, `Lab_Acquaintance`): a cramped hollow he dug under the prison — rough rock, the cells' foundation
blocks overhead, a ceiling of slabs on old timber braces, candle stubs burning in niches cut in the rock. At the west
end, **his hoard** as a humble merchant would keep it: rolled carpets tied with cord and a sack of spice (58 art, the
sack ~0.5 m), and a brass lamp with a hookah on his bedroll (38). The model also painted an iron-bound strongbox with a
padlock (it would read as a reward chest the hero can't open) and a golden figurine that came out as a real-world
religious statue: both cut out.

The way back up is at `SlabDoor`: a **hole in the ceiling** where the slab of his cell has been moved, daylight from the
cell falling in, a knotted rope hanging from it down a rock pillar to two stacked crates (the step up). No ladder (D772),
no cut floor (B201): the floor runs whole under the crates. The rope's end was a loop that read as a noose; it is
painted over with the pillar's stone (`PATCH` in the script), the knot stays.

The wall tile is the right part of the generation and its mirror (328 art wide): the generation's middle was a dark
arched passage (it would read as an exit) and its left a barred cell window. The mirror makes some blocks symmetric;
it is darkened more than the other walls (×0.65) to keep them quiet.
