# Alchemist's Corridor planes (M6d-1)

**AI-generated.** Made with `design/shots/candidates/m6d1/m6d1.py` on top of `Scripts/Tools/comfy_backdrop.py`, using
**Qwen-Image** (Alibaba Qwen, Apache 2.0) with the Qwen-Image-Lightning LoRA (lightx2v, Apache 2.0). Brought down ×3 to
the game's pixel and a few colours (octree); the wall is darkened toward violet. The floor is `../Lab/gen_slab.png`
(already the placeholder's). A Steam release has to disclose AI-generated content. Review:
`design/shots/m6d1_lab_rooms.png` (frames 1–3).

The room (`Maps/AlchemistCorridor.tscn`): side branch, 3 cells (1728 × 320 art = 2592 × 480 world) at [15,2] on layer 2,
above floor 1 of the Laboratory; floor y 384 world (art row 256), camera x 0…1152 art, y fixed. The alchemist's own
gallery, the Laboratory's violet stone and brass: a flat wall with three rows of small arched niches of vials (green and
amber), brass pipes with valves, oil lamps. What he works on stands along it: **the djinn experiment** — a man-sized
glass vessel in a riveted brass cage, chained and padlocked, a small fire swirling inside (the djinn burst out of a
prisoner in the cells, D304; this is what he keeps them in) —, a rack of small cages (most open and empty, a sleeping
desert lizard, a caged ember), and **his work table** — an alembic on a burner dripping into an amber flask, flasks,
mortar, books and a candle: the table the key lies on («left on the alchemist's table», `alchemist_key`).

Both ends are painted passages: the **stairs** at `StairsDoor` (an arched doorway in the Laboratory's stone, steps and
a lamp inside, opening 112) and the **storeroom door** at `StoreDoor` (dark oak, three iron bands, a black lock plate
with a keyhole, an iron ring, a blank brass plate over it; opening 112). The model drew the stairs rising twice (seeds
11101, 11102 «going down»); from here they lead down to floor 1 — the doorway says «stairs», not which way.
