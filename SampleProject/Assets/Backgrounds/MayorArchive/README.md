# Mayor's Archive planes (M6c-3)

**AI-generated.** Made with `design/shots/candidates/m6c3/m6c3.py` on top of `Scripts/Tools/comfy_backdrop.py`, using
**Qwen-Image** (Alibaba Qwen, Apache 2.0) with the Qwen-Image-Lightning LoRA (lightx2v, Apache 2.0). Brought down ×3 to
the game's pixel and a few colours (octree). The floor is `../Hall/gen_floor.png` (the Mayor Hall's marble with the red
runner). A Steam release has to disclose AI-generated content. Review: `design/shots/m6c3_archive_cistern.png` (frame 1).

The room (`Maps/MayorArchive.tscn`): interior, 1 cell (576 × 320 art = 864 × 480 world) at [29,4], over the Mayor Hall;
floor y 384 world (art row 256), one screen, the camera does not move. The mayor's private archive behind the bookshelf
(D773; «story: the empire and the barriers», world_structure_plan): dark shelves from floor to vault packed with
scrolls, bound ledgers and leather document cases, the palace's carved frieze with the plain golden disc (Q57), two red
banners as in the hall, a lattice window, wall candles. Story props: a **map of the land** on an easel — a desert
island ringed by mountains and sea (Khalahas, «closed in by mountains or seas», lore) with **four pale-blue domes over
villages** (the barriers), no writing; a **lectern** with an open ledger and a candle, scrolls tied with red cord at
its foot. The way back is the **back of the secret bookcase door**: rough planks, two iron strap hinges, an iron ring,
in a carved stone frame — the hall's side is the bookcase (`MayorHall/ArchiveDoor`, key `mayor_key`, which is not in the demo (D921), gate
`mayor_archive`).
