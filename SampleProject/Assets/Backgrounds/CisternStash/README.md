# Cistern Stash planes (M6c-3)

**AI-generated.** Made with `design/shots/candidates/m6c3/m6c3.py` on top of `Scripts/Tools/comfy_backdrop.py`, using
**Qwen-Image** (Alibaba Qwen, Apache 2.0) with the Qwen-Image-Lightning LoRA (lightx2v, Apache 2.0). Brought down ×3 to
the game's pixel and a few colours (octree); the wall is darkened toward violet. The floor is `../Cellar/gen_floor.png`
(already the placeholder's), the front is the Aqueduct's vault pier. A Steam release has to disclose AI-generated
content. Review: `design/shots/m6c3_archive_cistern.png` (frames 7–8).

The room (`Maps/CisternStash.tscn`): secret, 2 cells (1152 × 320 art = 1728 × 480 world) at [24,8], under the cistern's
east end; floor y 384 world (art row 256), camera x 0…576 art, y fixed. A **dry side chamber** of the old cistern: low
vaulted arches and empty niches, the old water lines high on the stones, a dry channel, roots through the joints,
cobwebs. Someone hides goods here: **sealed water jars** (cloth and wax, tied with rope — water is dearer than oil in
the City), a ladle and a copper pot at the west, crates with rolled carpets, sacks and a rope at the east. Whose stash
it is the lore does not say; nothing painted names it. No painted chest or strongbox: the model drew a padlocked box
twice, it is cut out so it can't be mistaken for the room's real reward.

The way out is the **breach seen from inside** at `ExitDoor`: a ragged hole broken through fitted blocks, the cistern's
water glinting beyond (opening 112).
