# Tavern Attic planes (M6c-2)

**AI-generated.** Made with `design/shots/candidates/m6c2/m6c2.py` on top of `Scripts/Tools/comfy_backdrop.py`, using
**Qwen-Image** (Alibaba Qwen, Apache 2.0) with the Qwen-Image-Lightning LoRA (lightx2v, Apache 2.0). Brought down ×3 to
the game's pixel and a few colours (octree). The floor is `../ElderHouse/gen_floor.png`, whole; the ladder is
`../Rooftops/gen_ladder.png`. A Steam release has to disclose AI-generated content. Review:
`design/shots/m6c2_rooftops.png` (frames 5–6).

The room (`Maps/TavernAttic.tscn`): interior, 2 cells (1152 × 320 art = 1728 × 480 world) at [23,4], over the market's
east end; floor y 384 world (art row 256), flat, camera x 0…576 art, y fixed. The hero climbs up from behind the market
stall (`City/TavernAtticDoor`, «драбина за прилавком») through the **hatch**: the ladder's top stands 40 art above the
whole floor at `LadderDoor` (B201: no hole cut, the ladder stands against the floor). The tavern keeps its stores up
here: wine barrels on a rack, sacks of flour and grain on crates with a broken chair, onions, garlic and sausages
hanging by a lantern. Lore: the merchant's lost ledger is somewhere in it (world_structure_plan) — its pickup is the
room routine's; the sacks at the east end are a natural place.
