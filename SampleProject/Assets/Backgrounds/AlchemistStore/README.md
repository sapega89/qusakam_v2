# Alchemist's Storeroom planes (M6d-1)

**AI-generated.** Made with `design/shots/candidates/m6d1/m6d1.py` on top of `Scripts/Tools/comfy_backdrop.py`, using
**Qwen-Image** (Alibaba Qwen, Apache 2.0) with the Qwen-Image-Lightning LoRA (lightx2v, Apache 2.0). Brought down ×3 to
the game's pixel and a few colours (octree); the wall is darkened toward violet. The door is the corridor's
(`../AlchemistCorridor/gen_door.png`, same door from inside), the floor `../Lab/gen_slab.png`, the front the corridor's
herb beam. A Steam release has to disclose AI-generated content. Review: `design/shots/m6d1_lab_rooms.png` (frames 4–5).

The room (`Maps/AlchemistStore.tscn`): interior behind a key door, 2 cells (1152 × 320 art = 1728 × 480 world) at
[18,2] on layer 2; floor y 384 world (art row 256), camera x 0…576 art, y fixed. A cramped storeroom: dark shelves to
a low stone vault, wax-sealed clay jars, bottles of coloured liquid, small boxes, bundles of drying herbs, cobwebs.
On the floor: two small barrels (32 art, D814), a big bottle in a wicker basket and a crate of flasks in straw; at the
east end a **spare djinn vessel** — the same caged glass sphere as in the corridor, empty, lying on its cradle with a
copper pipe. The model put it beside a crate with two padlocks and a chain: that reads as a reward chest and is cut out
(like the Cistern Stash's strongbox). The wall's small boxes have brass rings; at ×0.85 and darkened they read as shelf
stock, not chests.

The wall tile is the right part of the generation and its mirror (472 art wide): the model's seam left translucent
ghosts of shelves at its middle.
