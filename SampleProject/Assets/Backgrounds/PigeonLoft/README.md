# Pigeon Loft planes (M6c-2)

**AI-generated.** Made with `design/shots/candidates/m6c2/m6c2.py` on top of `Scripts/Tools/comfy_backdrop.py`, using
**Qwen-Image** (Alibaba Qwen, Apache 2.0) with the Qwen-Image-Lightning LoRA (lightx2v, Apache 2.0). Brought down ×3 to
the game's pixel and a few colours (octree). The floor is `../ElderHouse/gen_floor.png`, whole. A Steam release has to
disclose AI-generated content. Review: `design/shots/m6c2_rooftops.png` (frame 4).

The room (`Maps/PigeonLoft.tscn`): secret, 1 cell (576 × 320 art = 864 × 480 world) at [20,3], over the Rooftops; floor
y 384 world (art row 256), one screen, the camera does not move. It is the inside of the **dovecote** that stands on
the roofs: warm mud-brick walls lined with arched nesting niches, perches across them, pigeons, small openings high up
with daylight, feathers in the air. The hero came in by breaking the boards on its door («розбити дошки»), so the way
out is that **doorway from inside** — broken planks hanging from its frame, the City's roofs and domes in daylight
through it — in the middle of the screen at `ExitDoor`. No ladder, no hole in the floor. The pigeon keeper's things
(a grain sack, a wicker basket, a stool) stand at the left; the floor right of the door (`SecretArea_Loft`, x 640) is
left clear for the secret's reward.
