# Collapsed Drain planes (M6d-1)

**AI-generated.** Made with `design/shots/candidates/m6d1/m6d1.py` on top of `Scripts/Tools/comfy_backdrop.py`, using
**Qwen-Image** (Alibaba Qwen, Apache 2.0) with the Qwen-Image-Lightning LoRA (lightx2v, Apache 2.0). Brought down ×3 to
the game's pixel and a few colours (octree); the far and middle planes are darkened toward violet. The floor is
`../Cellar/gen_floor.png` (already the placeholder's), the front the Aqueduct's vault pier. A Steam release has to
disclose AI-generated content. Review: `design/shots/m6d1_lab_rooms.png` (frames 8–12).

The room (`Maps/CollapsedDrain.tscn`): underground, 7 cells (4032 × 320 art = 6048 × 480 world) at [12,8] on layer 2,
under the whole Laboratory; floor y 384 world (art row 256), camera x 0…3456 art, y fixed. The Laboratory's old brick
drain, partly fallen in: far tunnels of brick vaults with channels of faintly glowing green water and broken pipes
(×0.45), an arcade of brick arches on piers with rusted pipes, roots and fallen bricks (×0.75), two **heaps of the
collapse** with a standing piece of arch and a snapped pipe (×1.0, 96 art, the second mirrored), and the story of the
place: **a broken brass pipe from the Laboratory pouring green sludge into a stone basin**, broken vials beside it
(×0.9) — what the alchemist pours away.

Both ends are painted shafts up, rungs in a brick pillar from the vault to the walk line, standing before the wall (no
cut floor, B201; rungs where a drain would really have them, D772): the **hatch** into the cells at `HatchDoor` (a
banded wooden hatch closing the top) and the **grate** in floor 3 at `GrateDoor` (an iron grate on top, lamp light from
the Laboratory falling through).
